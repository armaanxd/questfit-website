# QuestFit: brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** On 2026-11-04 the owner was offered the eight-question interview and replied "continue". Everything below is an authored decision, not a quotation. Evidence comes from the app source (`~/Projects/questfit/game.js`, `jdvoice.js`) and the current site copy; assumptions are marked.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Game HUD, earned warmth, dry humour. References: the companion who warms up in Persona's social links; a Monster Hunter quest board; the stat screen of any RPG you played too long. (Authored.)
2. **Journey.** Land inside the app on Day 1 with a grumpy guide. Clear your first quest. Pick a class. Choose what a missed day costs. Train for two weeks in a few scrolls. Hit level 16 and watch the guide finally say he's proud. See the real app. Start your own campaign, and the guide is grumpy again. (Authored.)
3. **Energy.** Calm open, rising through the grind, one near-silent beat at level 15, loudest at level 16, quiet and wry at the close.
4. **Feeling, and the one moment.** Curiosity, a small win, ownership, unease, momentum, held breath, pride, trust, resolve. The moment: JD, who ignored you the whole way down, does a fist pump and says he's proud of you.
5. **What no other site does.** Your scroll *is* the training. The HUD levels you up from 1 to 16 using the app's real rules (250 XP a level, the real quest rewards), and the guide's attitude thaws on the app's real tiers as you go.
6. **Range.** Playful-dense: a game HUD, information-forward, not premium-minimal. No violet-to-blue gradients or glow (the previous site leaned on both).
7. **World or scenes.** Distinct scenes. They are screens of one app, not places in one world.
8. **Assets.** Owner's own only, no generation, no kie.ai spend: JD (VRM model plus four attitude stills), four app screenshots, the 30-second app tour video, the real game rules and JD's real lines.

## What, who, believe, do

- **What:** QuestFit, an Android fitness app that turns daily workouts into an RPG campaign. For people who quit plans because nothing makes them come back.
- **Believe:** "Showing up every day is the game, and the game notices."
- **Do next:** one action, one label everywhere: **Start your campaign**. The app is not on Google Play yet, so the button says so honestly.

## Grammar

**Live surface.** The demo is the argument: the page behaves like the app (HUD, tabs, quest board, stat sheet), runs real logic, and says on its face that the campaign is a demo.

## Journey and feeling curve

```
1  Today        Curiosity   Day 1 board, JD aloof; your scroll runs the main quest timer to 30 min
2  Classes      Ownership   four classes travel past sideways; picking one rewrites your board
3  Stakes       Unease      a real control: what does a missed day cost you
4  The grind    Momentum    days tick, eight stats fill, JD goes grudging, then warm
5  Level 15     Held breath almost nothing: one line, 10 XP short (authored silence)
6  Level 16     Pride       the frame opens on JD, fist pump, "Proud of you."  ← PEAK
7  The app      Trust       the real screens and the real 30-second tour, help panel
8  Your turn    Resolve     an input: name your campaign, JD resets to aloof
```

**Peak (act 6):** "JD ignored me the whole way down, then I hit level 16 and he did a fist pump and said he was proud of me."

**Tell-someone sentence:** It's the site where scrolling levels you up, and the grumpy guide slowly starts to like you.

**Authored silence:** act 5 is deliberately near-empty. It is not dead scroll.

## Honesty rules

- Every number is computed from the app's real rules (`game.js`: 250 XP per level; Warrior board 100 + 50 + 40 + 80 = 270 XP and 53 coins a day). Labelled "Demo campaign" in the HUD.
- No invented user counts, ratings or downloads.
