Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Draft. This policy hasn't been reviewed yet and isn't final.

The short version

QuestFit keeps your campaign and your health data on your phone. There are no accounts, and the app doesn't send your progress, quests, stats or health data to us or anyone else.

What the app stores

To run your campaign, QuestFit saves the following on your device only:

  • The name you enter, your class, your fitness level and your penalty mode.
  • Your quests, progress, XP, coins, streaks, stats and recent history.
  • Your timezone, so the daily quest board resets at your local midnight.

The email and passphrase on the sign-in screen are not saved or sent anywhere.

Health Connect

If you choose to connect Health Connect, QuestFit asks to read three kinds of data:

  • Steps, to count toward step quests.
  • Exercise sessions (workouts), to count toward timed quests.
  • Heart rate during those workouts, only to check that a workout really happened.

QuestFit uses this data only to verify your quests. It never writes to Health Connect, never sends the data off your phone, never sells or shares it, and never uses it for ads. It keeps a small record of each verified quest (for example "6,200 steps, from Health Connect") in your campaign on your phone.

Connecting is optional. Without it, you can complete every main quest with the in-app timer.

To stop access, open Health Connect, go to App permissions, choose QuestFit and turn its permissions off. You can also delete QuestFit's data there.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only if you turn on quest reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.
  • Internet: used to load fonts and workout guides. Your campaign data is never sent.

Other services

  • Google Fonts: the app and this website load fonts from Google, which receives your IP address as part of a normal web request.
  • YouTube: "Find video guide" opens a YouTube search. YouTube's own privacy policy applies there.

QuestFit has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Deleting your data

Use the reset button in the app, or uninstall QuestFit. Either removes everything, because nothing is stored anywhere else.

Children

QuestFit isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

Planned features such as the food log and cloud backup will change what QuestFit handles. This policy will be updated, and you'll be asked for permission, before any of them reaches the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact.questfit@gmail.com
